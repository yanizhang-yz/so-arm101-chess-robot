# Kid Chess — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-07-16

Kid Chess is a chess game made for young children. We built it to be private by
design. This policy explains, in plain words, what that means.

## The short version

**Kid Chess collects nothing, sends nothing, and tracks nobody.** There are no
accounts, no sign-in, no ads, and no analytics. The game works with the device
in Airplane Mode.

## What we collect

Nothing. Kid Chess does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
information — not your child's name, age, location, contacts, photos, voice, nor
any device or usage identifiers.

- **No account and no login.** You never create a profile.
- **No analytics or advertising.** We include no third-party analytics,
  advertising, or tracking software of any kind.
- **No network use.** The game contains no code that contacts the internet.
  Everything — the chess rules, the computer opponents, the sounds, and the
  spoken hints — runs entirely on the device.

Because the app collects no data, there is no data to sell, and none is ever
sold or shared.

## Sound and speech

Kid Chess speaks its hints aloud using the **device's own built-in
text-to-speech** and makes music with the browser's built-in sound engine. No
audio is recorded, and the microphone is never used. The app does not request
microphone, camera, or location permission.

## Children's privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Kid Chess is intended for children and is designed to comply with the U.S.
Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. Since the app
collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, there
is nothing to request parental consent for.

## Purchases

Kid Chess has no in-app purchases and no external links. Nothing in the app costs
money or leaves the app.

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same
